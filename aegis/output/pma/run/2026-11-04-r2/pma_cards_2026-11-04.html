What this is. The CFTC publishes every week how traders are positioned in US futures (the Commitments of Traders report). Large speculators are the hedge funds and trend-following funds. Crowded is a warning, not a signal: when everyone is already in, the next surprise forces them out together, so crowded longs fall hardest on bad news and crowded shorts squeeze on good news.
