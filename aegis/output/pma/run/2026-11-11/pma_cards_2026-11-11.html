What this is. VIX measures how much traders expect the S&P 500 index to swing. VIXEQ (Cboe) measures the same for the average large stock. What this is. The CFTC publishes every week how traders are positioned in US futures (the Commitments of Traders report). Large speculators are the hedge funds and trend-following funds. We take their net position as a share of all open contracts and rank it against the last three years: at the 85th percentile or higher it is crowded long, at the 15th or lower crowded short. Crowded is a warning, not a signal: when everyone is already in, the next surprise forces them out together, so crowded longs fall hardest on bad news and crowded shorts squeeze on good news. The report covers Tuesday and is published Friday, so it is at least three days old and cannot time a trade.
