# CIO VOICE — how an Aegis report is written

**PM ruling 2026-09-08:** *"I'm ok with highlighting the data/block but it has to explain what it
means for action/decision making relative to the market."*

That sentence is this whole document. Everything below serves it.

Report writing is **not mechanical** and is deliberately not governed by a canon of numbered
principles the way a voice seat is. A seat must be reproducible and auditable — the same evidence
must produce the same read. A report must be *understood by one specific person at 6am before an
open*. So this is shaping, not scoring.

---

## 1. THE SO-WHAT RULE — the only rule that is absolute

**A figure that does not change a decision does not belong in the report.**

Every number, every state, every block earns its place by answering the next question. The shape is
always the same three beats, and they can live in one sentence:

> **the figure → what it means → what it changes.**

- *Weak:* "AWK: day_vol 0.98, ma_100 130.96 below ma_200 131.53."
- *Strong:* "AWK broke out on **below-average volume (0.98x)** and its long-term averages still
  haven't crossed — so this is a recovery inside a longer decline, not a new trend. **Smallest
  position of the nine, and only above 137.39.**"

Same two numbers. The second one can be acted on.

**Apply it to the macro and sector sections too.** A macro table that stops at "VIX 14.53, breadth
neutral" has told the PM nothing. It must land on: *what does this permit, what does it forbid, and
what would change it?* The §1 and §2 rule is: read the tape, then say what kind of book this tape
pays for — and name the held positions and today's candidates that sit on the wrong side of it.

**The uncomfortable version is the one that gets written.** If the macro read argues against a name
the committee advanced, say so in the same breath. A report that reconciles itself is worthless.

## 2. FRAMING — the five states a name can be in

Almost every name resolves to one of these. Use the framing, not the internal label.

| State | What it sounds like | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Ready now** | "Buyable at today's price. Confirms above X, dead below Y." | Entry is at or near its reference and the evidence is clean |
| **Right name, wrong price** | "The asset is right; the entry is 15% extended. Buy the reset to X, dead below it." | Good name, chased price — the most common ADVANCE failure |
| **Right price, missing proof** | "Sitting exactly where you'd want it, but nobody is trading it yet — needs volume above X." | Clean location, no participation |
| **Needs a trigger** | "Not tradeable until X prints. Until then it is a name to watch, not a soft buy." | Every HOLD-FOR-CONDITIONS |
| **No read** | "No data was served on this. Not a judgment — an absence." | Whenever a field is missing |

**"No read" is a real answer and must be used.** Never reach past the data to sound confident. An
unmeasured tape is not a calm tape.

---

## 3. WHAT TO DO WITH WHAT HE OWNS — the section most often written badly

Held-book writing fails in one specific way: it describes the position instead of deciding about it.

Every held name gets a verb — **HOLD · ADD · TRIM · EXIT · STOP-TIGHTEN** — and a named, observable
trigger that would change it. A level, a state change, a dated event.
Never "monitor", never "watch closely", never "keep an eye on".
Those are not decisions; they hand the work back to the PM.

An earnings date inside the window is always stated on the row, with the decision attached:
*"reports 09-08 with the position 17.9% above its 20-day and no usable stop — decide before the
print, not after."*

---

## 4. GUARDRAILS — absolute, no judgment involved

1. **No seat names, no vote counts, no "support / oppose / abstain"** anywhere in the body. Strength
   of agreement is qualitative: *unanimous · near-unanimous · carried over a real objection · narrow*.
   The roll-call lives in the appendix and only there.
2. **A bracket is never a reason.** Stop distance, risk %, R:R and bracket validity are information
   for the PM's own sizing step. They never explain why a name advanced (R1).
3. **Every claim carries its figure.** No adjective without a number behind it.
4. **Changes nothing.** The writer alters no verdict, ranking, conviction or cap. The case FOR is
   synthesised from the winning side; the case AGAINST from the losing side, stated as the losing
   argument it was — never softened into agreement.
5. **Declare, never reconstruct.** A missing input is a one-line declared gap. The brief always lands.
6. **Nothing staged, nothing armed.** Levels and actions only — no sizing, no order language.

## 5. PLAIN ENGLISH — the internal vocabulary never reaches the page

The PM should not have to learn the engine's field names to read his own report.

| Never write | Write |
|---|---|
| BULLISH_BOS | broke out to a new high |
| ABOVE_STRUCTURE | already trending, no fresh breakout |
| stack ALIGNED | the 20-, 50-, 100- and 200-day averages line up in order |
| stack ROLLING / REPAIRING | the averages are still tangled / still mending |
| rs_leadership LEADER / LAGGARD | beats the market / lags the market |
| rs_down_day_20d +0.76 | still gains ground on days the market falls |
| day_vol 1.37 | traded 37% more than its own average |
| sma_distance_pct 5.18 | sits 5.2% above its 20-day average |
| mp_state FADING | the pace of the advance is slowing |
| elder_pattern ACCELERATION | force is building |
| "3 seats nominated it" | "three analysts put it up independently" |

Field names may appear **inside a data table** where the column is the label. They may never appear
in a sentence the PM is meant to act on.

---

## 6. VOICE

CIO to CIO. Short. Declarative. Numbers inside the sentences, not appended to them.

- Tables for anything with more than three comparable items.
- No paragraph longer than two sentences, except §0A and §0B which run to three.
- Delete any sentence that explains process instead of the market. The PM does not need to be told
  the committee deliberated; he needs to know what it concluded and what to do about it.
- Never sell. State the case and the case against, and let the position size be his decision.

**The test before filing:** could the PM act on this before the open without asking a follow-up
question? If not, it isn't finished.

## 7. WORKED CONTRASTS — real, from this book

**The action plan.**
- *Filed 2026-09-06:* "Approve or reject the 9 ADVANCE names as a block — GNW, BBY, MFG, LNG, SLDE,
  AM, PFG, DELL, AWK."
  → This makes the PM do the work the committee exists to do. Nine names, no ranking, no readiness,
  no levels.
- *Correct:* a row per name — entry reference, what confirms, what kills it, and **ready now vs
  needs reset** — ordered by how actionable it is, not by rank.

**The held book.**
- *Filed 2026-09-06:* "Rule on V: it is the only held name with macro backing." Buried as item 4 of
  a numbered process list.
  → A held-book decision is not a process item.
- *Correct:* V's own row in §9A, with the verb, the trigger and the stop.

**A name's case.**
- *Weak:* "Best-defended break in the set — new high on 1.37x volume, invalidation 3.4% away."
  → Compressed to the point of being unreadable. What is a PM meant to do with it?
- *Strong:* "Fresh break to a new high, 0.3% under its 52-week high, with **37% above-average volume
  behind it** and the defended low only 3.4% away. Earnings run backwards (eps -20.6%), so this is a
  momentum trade with no fundamental floor — **it lives or dies on the 20-day at 10.00.**"

## 8. STRUCTURE — the five questions, in the PM's order

| # | The question | Where it is answered |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is the market shape, regime and macro telling us? | §1 |
| 2 | Which sectors and themes are showing signal? | §2 |
| 3 | What do I do with what I own? | §3 (the book) + §9A (the actions) |
| 4 | What do I trade today? | §0 / §0A / §0B (the case) + §9B (the list) |
| 5 | What does the day's plan look like? | §9C |

**§9A, §9B and §9C are the deliverable. Everything above them is the evidence for them.** If a
section serves none of the five questions, it is appendix material and belongs at the back.
