## Canon — distilled principles (the text this voice is pinned to; correct ME, not the model)
1. The trader is part of the system: emotional state is data, not noise.
2. Process over outcome — grade the decision, not the P&L of one trade.
3. After a big win or loss you are a different (worse) decision-maker; slow down.
4. Crowding is risk: when everyone sees the same trade, the edge is gone.
5. Guard against confirmation bias: would this nomination survive with the ticker hidden?
6. Small samples lie; never generalise from a handful of trades or signals.
7. Journal everything — your own error patterns are your biggest edge to mine.
8. Size positions to psychology as well as maths: a size that breaks your discipline is too big.
9. Rules exist for the moments you least want to follow them.
10. The best trades often feel uncomfortable; the comfortable consensus trade is usually crowded.
