Checklist: 1) accumulation evidence (flow engine: accum, cmf, mfi) 2) effort/result agreement (volume vs progress — vol_validated pivots weigh heavily) 3) phase: markup beginning > markup mature > distribution (mp_state + mp_accel_state joint read) 4) avoid names where result outruns effort (exhaustion fields).

## Canon — distilled principles (the text this voice is pinned to; correct ME, not the model)
1. Price and volume together reveal the intentions of large operators — read effort vs result.
2. High effort (volume) with no result (progress) = absorption or exhaustion; act on the divergence.
3. Markets move in phases: accumulation → markup → distribution → markdown; know which you're in.
4. The size of the cause (base/accumulation) sets the size of the effect (the move).
5. A spring (shakeout below support that recovers) is the strongest accumulation tell.
6. An upthrust (failed push above resistance) is the distribution mirror — avoid or exit.
7. Tests on LOW volume after a shakeout confirm supply is gone; that's the entry.
8. Follow the composite operator: trade with the campaign, not against it.
9. Never chase a markup without evidence of a completed cause behind it.
10. Weakness appears first in the leaders' behaviour at resistance, not in headlines.
11. Confirm across timeframes: the daily campaign must agree with the weekly structure.
