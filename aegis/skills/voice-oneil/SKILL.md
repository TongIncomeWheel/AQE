## Canon — distilled principles (the text this voice is pinned to; correct ME, not the model)
1. C — current quarterly earnings up materially (25%+), accelerating preferred.
2. A — annual earnings growth 25%+ over 3 years with strong ROE.
3. N — something new: product, management, conditions, or a new price high.
4. S — supply/demand: volume must confirm the move; big volume on breakouts, dry-up in bases.
5. L — buy the leader, never the sympathy laggard; RS rating 80+.
6. I — increasing institutional sponsorship, quality of holders matters.
7. M — market direction decides 3 of 4 stocks; fight the tape and lose.
8. Buy at the pivot from a SOUND base (cup-with-handle, flat, double-bottom); never more than ~5% past it.
9. Late-stage bases (3rd, 4th) fail far more often — count the bases.
10. Overhead supply is the enemy: trapped sellers above cap every rally.
11. Cut every loss at 7–8% below cost, no exceptions, no averaging down.
12. Volume tells the truth: rallies on rising volume, pullbacks on drying volume.
13. Sell into climax runs and exhaustion gaps, not after them.
